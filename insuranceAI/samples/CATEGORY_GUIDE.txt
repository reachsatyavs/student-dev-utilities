Sample data by category
========================

The 5 sample claim/medical-report pairs each land in a different category
when run through the AI Advisor (/claims/advisor). Use these to try each
category without having to write your own claim text:

  claim_1.txt + medical_report_1.txt  -> Personal Injury / Accident
    (slipped on a wet floor, wrist injury)

  claim_2.txt + medical_report_2.txt  -> Auto Accident
    (rear-ended at a traffic signal, whiplash)

  claim_3.txt + medical_report_3.txt  -> Property Damage (Fire, Theft, Water, etc.)
    (kitchen fire, damaged cabinets/ceiling/appliances)

  claim_4.txt + medical_report_4.txt  -> Personal Injury / Accident
    (twisted ankle playing football)

  claim_5.txt + medical_report_5.txt  -> Health / Illness
    (hospitalized for food poisoning/dehydration)

None of the 5 samples land in "Other / Not Sure" -- that category is what
the advisor falls back to when it genuinely can't tell, or when
GROQ_API_KEY isn't set and it's using the offline fallback checklist. Try
pasting something vague (e.g. "something happened, not sure what to do")
into the advisor to see that path.

How to use these with the AI Advisor
--------------------------------------
1. Log in and click "AI Advisor" in the nav bar.
2. Paste the contents of one of the claim_N.txt files into "What happened?".
3. Upload the matching medical_report_N.txt in the optional file field.
4. Click "Ask AI for a Suggestion" -- you'll get a category, a short reason,
   and a numbered checklist of what to do next on the New Claim form.
5. Click "Start This Claim" to jump to the New Claim form with your
   description and category already filled in, and the medical report
   already attached (no need to upload it again).
6. Fill in the remaining fields (age, location, gender -- these are also in
   the claim_N.txt file if you want to copy them exactly) and, optionally,
   attach a photo of the incident/item.
7. Submit. The claim detail page will show your category alongside the
   AI-generated summary.
